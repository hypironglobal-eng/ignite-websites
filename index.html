About IGNITE

Why IGNITE Was Created

Every year, thousands of students pass their exams but step into adulthood without direction. IGNITE was created to close that gap — bringing structured leadership training, mentorship, and career guidance directly into the school system, so growth does not stop at the classroom door.

The Problem We Are Solving

Across many Nigerian secondary schools, students receive strong academic instruction but very little structured support for character, leadership, and career decision-making. Left unaddressed, this gap produces graduates who are qualified on paper but unprepared for real responsibility.

Our Vision

A generation of young Nigerians who lead with discipline, purpose, and integrity — equipped to shape their communities and contribute meaningfully to national development.

Our Mission

To build a sustainable, school-based system that guides students through leadership training, mentorship, and career orientation, turning potential into practical, lifelong readiness.

What IGNITE Means

IGNITE reflects exactly what the programme sets out to do: spark the discipline, confidence, and sense of purpose that students carry with them long after they leave the classroom.

Why Schools Matter

Schools are where habits, character, and direction are formed. Working inside the school system allows IGNITE to reach students consistently, over time, rather than through a single event that fades quickly.

Why Sustainability Matters

Real change in a young person takes more than one session — it takes a system. IGNITE is built to continue continuously, so schools, sponsors, and government partners can measure lasting impact, not just short-term activity.

The Programme

Programme Overview

IGNITE runs as a structured, in-school programme that combines regular training sessions, mentorship, and practical projects to build student leadership, discipline, and career clarity over a full term.

Who the Programme Is For

The pilot is designed for secondary school students from JSS3 to SS3 — the stage where career decisions and leadership habits begin to take shape.

Programme Modules

1. Self-Leadership & Discipline

2. Values & Responsibility

3. Communication & Confidence

4. Leadership & Teamwork

5. SDG & Civic Responsibility

6. Career Pathway Guidance

7. Community Service Planning

8. Reflection & Reporting

How It Works (Delivery Model)

  • Weekly Training: In-school sessions led by trained facilitators focusing on specific growth areas.
  • Teachers & Counsellors: Support the programme inside the school, reinforcing learning between sessions.
  • School Liaison Officers: Coordinate scheduling, attendance, and communication with the school.
  • NYSC SDGs CDS Group: Support facilitation and connect the programme to national SDG advocacy.
  • Guest Speakers & Mentors: Provide real-world exposure across different career paths.
  • Monitoring & Evaluation: Progress tracked via attendance, participation, and assessments to produce impact reports.

Helping Students Choose Careers That Fit Their Strengths and Future

Many students choose career paths because of pressure, trend, parental expectation, peer influence, or lack of exposure. IGNITE helps students understand themselves, explore career options, and make better decisions about their future.

Bring IGNITE to Your School

Schools do not pay to participate in the programme. Schools serve as implementation partners by providing student access, a teacher or counsellor representative, time for programme sessions, venue support, and cooperation with the IGNITE team.

What Schools Provide

  • Approval from school leadership
  • 30 selected students
  • Teacher or counsellor representative
  • Space for sessions
  • Support for attendance and monitoring

What IGNITE Provides

  • Programme structure & materials
  • Facilitator coordination
  • Career pathway guidance & SDG orientation
  • Monitoring tools & Reporting
