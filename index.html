About IGNITE Leadership Club

A school-based youth development and orientation system built to close the gap between academic achievement and real-world readiness for Nigerian secondary school students.

The Challenge

Why IGNITE Was Created

Every year, thousands of students pass their exams but step into adulthood without direction. IGNITE was created to close that gap â€” bringing structured leadership training, mentorship, and career guidance directly into the school system, so growth does not stop at the classroom door.

Across many Nigerian secondary schools, students receive strong academic instruction but very little structured support for character, leadership, and career decision-making. Left unaddressed, this gap produces graduates who are qualified on paper but unprepared for real responsibility.

Our Vision

A generation of young Nigerians who lead with discipline, purpose, and integrity â€” equipped to shape their communities and contribute meaningfully to national development.

Our Mission

To build a sustainable, school-based system that guides students through leadership training, mentorship, and career orientation, turning potential into practical, lifelong readiness.

Delivery Model

  • Weekly Training: In-school sessions led by trained facilitators focusing on specific growth areas.
  • Teachers & Counsellors: Support the programme inside the school, reinforcing learning between sessions.
  • School Liaison Officers: Coordinate scheduling, attendance, and communication with the school.
  • NYSC SDGs CDS Group: Support facilitation and connect the programme to national SDG advocacy.
  • Guest Speakers & Mentors: Provide real-world exposure across different career paths.
  • Monitoring & Evaluation: Progress tracked via attendance, participation, and assessments to produce impact reports.
